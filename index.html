The Story Behind Dhanoka

Ashok Singh Yadav

Coming from a farming background, Ashok Singh Yadav understood that farmers often face challenges not only in cultivation, but also in accessing agricultural inputs at reasonable prices and at the right time.

“Dhanoka is my effort to make access to agricultural inputs a little simpler and more affordable for the people who work closest to the land.”
